- **Backup destinations.** Backups go to named destinations: a local folder, SFTP (password or key, with the host key confirmed on first use), FTP with explicit or implicit TLS, S3-compatible storage (AWS, Cloudflare R2, Wasabi, MinIO, Silo), Backblaze B2, Google Drive (account token or service account), Dropbox, OneDrive and SharePoint, WebDAV (Nextcloud, ownCloud) and any other rclone remote. Their credentials are encrypted with the key of the secret store and never shown again. A destination can encrypt archives and restic snapshots before they leave the server, with a key of its own; its recovery kit, downloaded after the password is confirmed, lets a new server read them (`v-import-user-backup`). Commands: `v-add-backup-destination`, `v-change-backup-destination`, `v-delete-backup-destination`, `v-check-backup-destination`, `v-list-backup-destination(s)`, `v-list-backup-destination-archives`, `v-export-backup-destination-key`.
- **Backup jobs.** A job backs up all users, the users of some packages or some users, with archives or restic snapshots, on a schedule (every hour, day, week or month, or a cron expression), and sends each backup to one or more destinations, each with its own retention. A destination that fails does not stop the others: the run ends `partial` and the administrators are notified. A dispatcher run from cron every minute starts the jobs that are due, catches up once after a pause and never starts a job twice; `BACKUP_PARALLEL` sets how many accounts are backed up at once across jobs, and packages set how often their accounts are backed up at most (`BACKUP_FREQUENCY`: hourly, daily or weekly; resellers stay within their own package). `BACKUP_REPORT` sends a mail on failures, a daily summary at 07:00, or nothing. Commands: `v-add-backup-job`, `v-change-backup-job`, `v-delete-backup-job`, `v-suspend-backup-job`, `v-unsuspend-backup-job`, `v-list-backup-job(s)`, `v-list-backup-job-runs`, `v-list-backup-schedule`, `v-run-backup-job`, `v-start-backup-job`, `v-update-backup-jobs`.
- The backup runs of a user and the archive list say which job made a backup and the state of each destination (`JOB`, `DESTS`, `DESTINATIONS`, `ENCRYPTED`); an archive can be deleted from one destination (`v-delete-user-backup USER ARCHIVE DEST`).
