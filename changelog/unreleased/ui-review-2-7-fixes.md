- A long list (the audit log has hundreds of pages) shows the first and the last page and two on each side of the current one, and the page links wrap on a phone.
- A switch is named by its title alone; the description, which screen readers also read as the description, is no longer part of its name.
- The PHP tab of a web domain has two different buttons, Save version and profile and Save PHP settings, instead of two Save buttons.
- The Doctor counts one warning, finding, problem or check in the singular ("1 failed, 1 warning", "1 finding"), on the pages and in the weekly digest.
